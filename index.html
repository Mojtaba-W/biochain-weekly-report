04 · Photonics

From Sensing to Control

How light-responsive biology inspires new technologies.

Light-sensitive proteins such as channelrhodopsins have enabled optogenetics: a research approach that uses light to influence activity in selected cells.

05 · Bioinspired Surfaces

Prevention Before Response

Lotus leaves, shark skin and insect wings inspire new surface designs.

Surface structure and material properties can change how water, particles and microorganisms interact with a material. Bioinspired surface engineering explores ways to reduce unwanted adhesion or contamination.
